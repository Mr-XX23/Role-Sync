# Negotiation plan

Goal: protect the deal's value and close on terms the business accepts.

## Steps
1. Get the facts: `search_deals` for the value, stage and close date; `recall` for the budget, decision process, pressure points and competitors; `search_catalog` for each item's price and **maximum discount**.
2. Set the limits: the target, the most we can give (never beyond the catalog limits without a manager's approval) and the walk-away point.
3. Look for pricing and packaging guidance with `search_knowledge_base`.

## The plan
- **Their likely asks**, and what is behind each one.
- **Give and get**: pair every concession with something in return (a longer term, more volume, a faster signature, a case study or reference, prepayment).
- **Order of concessions**: start with gives that cost us little and matter to them; keep discounts for last, in shrinking steps.
- **Walk-away**: the point where we pause, and what we say then.
- **Scripts**: an opening line, an answer to "can you do better?", and a close.

## Output
The plan on one page, with the numbers filled in. Never agree a price in writing without the rep's approval.
