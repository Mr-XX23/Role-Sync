# Discovery call prep

Goal: the rep walks into the call knowing the account, with a clear agenda and the right questions.

## Steps
1. Find the meeting with `list_calendar_events`: attendees, time, and any agenda in the invite.
2. Gather context all at once: `recall` about the account and any deal, `search_deals`, recent `search_emails` with the attendees' company, and `research_prospect` if little is known about them.
3. Work out each attendee's role and what they likely care about (the economic buyer, a daily user, a technical evaluator).
4. Pull one or two relevant case studies or proof points with `search_knowledge_base`.

## Output
- **Meeting**: the time in the rep's time zone, the attendees and their roles.
- **What we know**: two to four bullets. Mark assumptions as assumptions.
- **Goal of the call**: what a good outcome looks like, for example agreeing a next meeting with the economic buyer.
- **Agenda** for 30 minutes: introductions (3), their priorities (10), current situation and pain (10), fit and next steps (7).
- **Questions**: eight to ten open questions tailored to this account, grouped by pain, impact, process and decision. No yes-or-no questions.
- **Watch-outs**: likely objections or competitors, and how to respond.

Keep it to one page.
