# Competitive positioning

Goal: a practical plan for winning this deal against a named competitor.

## Steps
1. Find our battlecard for the competitor with `search_knowledge_base` (category BATTLECARD) and read it with `read_knowledge_document`.
2. Check recent public changes with `web_search`: pricing, launches, outages, acquisitions, reviews. Prefer sources from the last twelve months, and cite them.
3. `recall` what this account has said about the competitor, and check the deal's stage and decision criteria with `search_deals`.

## Output
- **Where we win**: three differentiators that matter to this customer, each with proof.
- **Where they are strong**: be honest, and say how to reframe or neutralize each point.
- **Discovery questions**: questions the rep can ask that bring the competitor's weak spots to light without naming them.
- **Traps to avoid**: claims we must not make, and topics that favor them.
- **Proof**: customers who chose us over them, or switched from them.
- **Talk track**: a short paragraph for when the customer asks "why not them?"

Stay factual and respectful: no disparaging claims, and mark anything that isn't verified.
